TRADITIONAL MALAYSIAN FOOD

A taste of Malaysia

Malaysian cuisine brings together flavours and cooking traditions from different communities. Popular dishes include nasi lemak, satay, roti canai, laksa and kuih tradisional.

Food is often part of celebrations, family gatherings and everyday life. Exploring local dishes is a delicious way to learn about the country's heritage.

CULTURAL HERITAGE

Many traditions, one Malaysia

Malaysia's communities celebrate different festivals, wear traditional clothing and preserve arts, music and crafts. Respecting these traditions helps visitors appreciate the country's cultural richness.
